No server? No problem

Works with just a USB stick

Don't have a Raspberry Pi? Use a USB stick as your "server". Plug it in, wait for the green message, and carry it to your other computer.

  1. On your laptop: install SyncBox, choose Through a USB stick and pick your stick.
  2. Share your folders. When you see ✔ Safe to unplug, everything is on the stick.
  3. On your PC: plug in the stick and double-click Install SyncBox.bat on it. Your folders arrive automatically.

Different drive letter on the other PC? No problem, SyncBox finds its stick by itself. A NAS or a shared network folder works the same way.

No Raspberry Pi? SyncBox also works with a USB stick, a NAS or a Google Drive folder as the server. In that case there's no website.

1. On the Raspberry Pi

git clone https://github.com/USER/syncbox
cd SyncBox
bash server/pi-setup.sh

You choose a password, and then your SyncBox runs at http://raspberrypi.local.

2. On each computer

Open http://raspberrypi.local, log in and click Download SyncBox for Windows. Run it and pick your folders. Done.

Questions

Do I need a Raspberry Pi?

No. A USB stick works too: SyncBox copies your changes onto the stick, tells you when it's safe to unplug, and your other computer picks them up when you plug it in there. A Raspberry Pi is handier because you never have to carry anything, and it gives you a website for your files.

Is it really free?

Yes. The software is open source (AGPL-3.0). On your own server it costs nothing. The hosted plans are only for people who don't want to run a server themselves.

Does it work when my computers are never on at the same time?

Yes, that's the whole idea. The server is always on, so your laptop leaves its changes there and your PC picks them up when it starts.

Can I sync my whole C: drive?

Yes, but Windows itself and installed programs are skipped, because those can't be copied between computers. Your user folder is synced separately, so it lands in the right place even if your username is different on the other PC.

Can I use it outside my home?

On your own server, install the free Tailscale app on the server and your computers to reach it from anywhere. Hosted plans work everywhere automatically.

Which systems are supported?

The SyncBox app is made for Windows 10 and 11. The server runs on any Linux, including Raspberry Pi OS. The website works in every modern browser, phones too.

What happens to my files if I stop using SyncBox?

Nothing. They're normal files in normal folders on your computers. SyncBox only copies them, it never locks them away.
